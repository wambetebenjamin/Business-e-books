SPIRITUAL EMPHASIS MONTH  ·  4TH WEEK  ·  WEDNESDAY
The Secret Place
The Three Levels of the Will of God
PRESBYTER JEREMIAH MUGALA
CHRICO CENTRAL CHURCH  ·  NAIROBI
THE INVITATION
There Is a Secret Place of the Most High
There is a secret place of the Most High — a place He has kept for those who draw near to Him.
He wants us to get closer — if possible, that we reach it. The invitation of this hour is not to visit His courts, but to come all the way in.
We have something that, when we balance it well, will give us victory in a great way.
He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
PSALM 91:1
THE STRUCTURE
The Three Levels of the Will of God
01
The Outer Court
Still in the will of God — yet a public place of noise, with a fence but no covering.
02
The Holy Place
Behind the curtain: complete darkness, three holy items, and the light of the Spirit.
03
The Holy of Holies
Where the Lord is. The place of worship, intercession, exchange and redefinition.
LEVEL 01
The Outer Court
The Outer Court is still in the will of God — though one who wants to do exploits has to be in the perfect will of God.
The Outer Court had no covering — but a fence.
In the Outer Court there is a lot of noise: unnecessary noise.
As it is a public place, there is no secret in the Outer Court.
LEVEL 02
The Holy Place
In the Holy Place there is a curtain, and complete darkness. There are three items in the Holy Place.
The candlestick — for lighting the candle, which represents the Holy Spirit.
Our life is enlightened by the Holy Spirit, for us to see the table of shewbread — unleavened bread with the twelve loaves.
The priests were to eat the bread, using the candles to see the bread.
LEVEL 02  ·  THE TABLE
The Bread of the Word
We use the Holy Spirit to light our spiritual bread — the Word of God.
The letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life.
2 CORINTHIANS 3:6
In the Holy Place the candles burn and there is no other light — and the shewbread.
LEVEL 03
The Holy of Holies
At the entrance there is another altar: the altar of incense.
It was a place to burn the most expensive perfumes — perfumes that cannot be used elsewhere.
This altar speaks of our deep worship and intercession.
The priest is covered by the smoke into the Holy of Holies.
LEVEL 03  ·  CONTINUED
Encounters in the Holy of Holies
That is where you exchange your weakness for strength. This is where the Lord is.
This is where Queen Esther reached a place of mentioning what she wanted.
It is a place of redefinition.
Solomon was in the Holy Place, and Solomon said he wants wisdom — which brings many other things with it.
The daughter of Herodias was there; she was asked what she wanted. She turned to her mother and asked for John the Baptist’s head. As it was a royal decree, he had to give it.
THE KEYS
For the Will of God

02

03

CLAIM THE PROMISE
The Promises of God
And it shall come to pass, that before they call, I will answer; and while they are yet speaking, I will hear.
ISAIAH 65:24
Come to God with this scripture, claiming the promise of God.
Call unto me, and I will answer thee, and shew thee great and mighty things, which thou knowest not.
JEREMIAH 33:3
God is inviting us to call Him, and He will answer — and He will tell us great things.
He is inviting us closer.
